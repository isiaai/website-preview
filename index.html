Our project

We are still in development – and still listening.

We don’t want to guess what people need. So right now we are looking for real pain points first – and building step by step with the people who will use it.

  1. Listen

    We talk to people who work with AI every day and collect the problems they really have.

  2. Sort

    We group the problems by situation, people and industry – and look for the easiest place to start.

  3. Build & test

    We combine our technologies into a product for one concrete problem and test it with a pilot group.

Digital fingerprint

Every AI interaction gets its own fingerprint. If something is changed afterwards, it no longer matches.

  • RecogniseShows what you contributed when working with AI.
  • ProveMakes your own work, and the work of others, traceable.
  • ExposeReveals changes and confirms what is original.
