The problem

AI helps every day. But two things are still hard.

1

Using AI well

The prompt that worked last month is lost in an old chat. Results vary from person to person, and nobody shows you how to get better.

2

Proving what’s yours

Your team lead or client asks how AI was used. Nobody can show which part of the work is really your own.

How it works

Two technologies. One for each problem.

Clarai runs in your browser while you use ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini as usual.

Solves: using AI well

Coaching

Better prompts. Better results.

Coaching gives you instant feedback on your prompts and shows you how to get better results from AI.

  • Better results, faster
  • Skills that grow
  • Less trial and error
Solves: proving what’s yours

Fingerprint

Your work, verified.

Fingerprint records each AI interaction while you work and seals it, so you can always show how a result came about. You see every entry and decide what you share.

  • Prove your contribution
  • Nothing to reconstruct
  • Private by default

Who we are

How do people and AI work well together?

Team photo coming soonOur team in Germany

ISIaAI is a project based in Germany. We are an interdisciplinary team from business, research and software development, united by one question: how can people and AI work well together?

AI is changing the way we work every day. We believe that in all of this, the human contribution should stay visible and valued. That’s what we’re working on, with our first product, Clarai.
